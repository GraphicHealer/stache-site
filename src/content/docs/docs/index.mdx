Stache helps you remember where your things are. Take a photo, put the item in a bin, and scan the bin's QR label when you need it again.

You do not have to name everything or build a perfect catalog. A photo and a place to put it are enough to get started. Add details when they are useful.

## Running Stache

Stache runs as one application with an embedded database; you do not need a separate database server. Your inventory stays on the machine where you host it unless you choose to connect an external service. Built-in recognition and photo similarity can run locally. AI suggestions can be reviewed, edited, or ignored.

For operational details, see [Backups and restore](/docs/extras/backups/), [Configuration](/docs/reference/configuration/), and [Security and access](/docs/reference/security/). If something is not working, start with [Troubleshooting and FAQ](/docs/reference/faq/).

Stache is open source. Browse the [application repository](https://github.com/GraphicHealer/Stache) for releases, issues, and source code.
