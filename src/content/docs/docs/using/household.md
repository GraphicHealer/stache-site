---
title: Household members and privacy
description: Share an inventory with household members while keeping selected items private.
---

Stache is designed for a shared household inventory. Items are **shared by default**, so everyone with an account can see the same areas and shared items. You can mark an individual item as private when it should only be visible to its owner.

## Create household accounts

The first person to create an account becomes the household administrator. After that, new accounts are created by someone who is already signed in:

1. Open **Settings → Household**.
2. Create an account for the person joining the household.
3. Give that person their sign-in credentials.

Public registration is closed after the initial account is created. Household members should use their own accounts rather than sharing one login, so Stache can record who made each change.

## Make an item private

Items are shared with the household unless you mark them **private**. A private item is visible only to its owner.

Private items are also excluded from external MCP connections. An assistant connected through MCP can work with shared household items, but it cannot see private items.

## Review an item's history

Stache records who filed an item, moved it, or changed its status. Open an item's history to see its activity and the account responsible for each change.

Changes can be undone. Undo follows the order of changes: if a newer change affects the same item, Stache asks you to undo that newer change first.
